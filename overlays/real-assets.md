# Overlay: real assets (ballast)

Use when the equity screen is empty or the user asked for gold, silver, industrial metals, fertilizer, or oil. This is a **waiting-room sleeve** for purchasing power, not a substitute for a great business at a sale-off.

Cash in the listing currency (USD T-bills, VND deposits) is a valid waiting room. Do not “do something” with commodities. Size so you can still buy stocks when a sale-off appears. No leverage, no 2x/3x, no option overlays.

## Instrument order

1. Physically allocated bullion ETF (or a metals/energy fund with transparent holdings) when you want the **price of the stuff**
2. Royalty/stream only if contracts, counterparties, and depletion are underwritable — then score as a **business** with `core.md`
3. First-quartile producer (AISC, reserve life, clean jurisdiction, modest debt at a **low** price deck) — `core.md` + this file
4. Avoid high-cost miners, explorers, juniors, leveraged ETFs

Small VND accounts: one or two liquid UCITS/US ETFs, not five thin London miners. State TER, custody, tracking, physical vs futures.

## Mid-cycle

Never value oil, urea, iron ore, copper, lithium on a top-quartile real-price year. State a low/mid/high real deck. Volume without a demand miracle. Sustaining capex; miners: closure/rehab.

Futures oil ETFs: show roll yield. Steep contango ⇒ skip or use a producer. Fertilizer: nitrogen vs potash vs phosphate are different cycles (peak urea P/E is a trap).

Gold/silver have **no earnings yield**. Hurdle is opportunity cost vs real rates and vs cash. Gold is insurance (repression, war, FX), not a 3-decimal DCF. A gold miner is high-beta, not “safer gold.” Silver: smaller sleeve. For a VND investor, gold is also USD/VND and real-rate — say so.

## Sizing

If equities are all watch/wait: bullion first, then optionally mid-cycle energy or copper. Fertilizer/lithium smaller. Do not stack gold ETF + gold miners + AUD + iron ore + oil (one risk-on commodity factor). Trim this sleeve when an equity name enters the good/very-good zone.

## Output

Why equities did not clear (or “user asked for ballast”); instrument table (ticker, physical/futures/producer, TER, liquidity, what you actually own); cycle trough/mid/peak; price deck; for producers: AISC/netback, net debt, FCF at the **low** deck, dilution; gold: real-rate and USD/VND, miner vs bullion; roll-yield warning; sleeve weights + trim rule that frees cash for a stock sale-off; hard avoids.
