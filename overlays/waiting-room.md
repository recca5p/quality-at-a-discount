# Overlay: waiting room (what is actually cheap)

Use **after** the equity screen is empty **and** `overlays/real-assets.md` has found that gold, silver, industrial metals, fertilizer, and oil/gas are **not** cheap ballast. This step is mandatory. “No stock to buy” is valid. “Nowhere to put cash” is not.

This is purchasing-power parking until a quality name enters the **good / very-good** zone. It is not a substitute business, not a crash timer, and not a license to buy cigar-butt equities.


Waiting-room is **temporary parking**, not a conclusion that equities never clear. Maintain the prior equity **Watch / shortlist** with price zones; when any name enters good/very-good or sale-off `true` / `moderately attractive`, **NOTIFY** the user and fund from this park (see router sale-off NOTIFY). “No stock to buy *today*” is valid; “always empty by construction” is a skill bug — escalate soft caps, do not deepen cash dogma.


## What “cheap” means here

Not a falling price. Not a 52-week low. The instrument that pays a **live contractual yield** in the spending currency (or a pack government bill after FX/tax) with principal that can fund the next QAD buy.

Klarman: cash is an option; MOS is required; do not stretch quality to “do something.” Marks: 2-sigma valuations plus euphoria is leave-cash, not buy-the-dip. Shiller CAPE is a **10-year return** signal, not a crash date (1999 vs 2021 vs 1966 all differ). Graham: a broken business at a low quote is a cigar, not accumulation.

1999 analog: CAPE ~44, 10y UST ~6.3%, gold ~$288 — residual cheap assets were **bonds and gold**. If gold has already rerated and real rates are positive, **deposits/bills remain**; do not force bullion.

## Rank (do not skip)

1. **Risk-free deposits in the spending currency.** VN spender: Big-4 **online** VND (VCB / BIDV / CTG / Agribank). Look up 3m / 6m / 12m separately. Counter/quầy rates are a different, worse product — do not mix. Do not chase a private-bank teaser (credit risk ≠ “cheap”).
2. **Pack government bills/bonds**, only if the user can actually hold them: US T-bills / 10y UST, UK gilt, DE Bund, AU ACGB. Haircut FX and tax. TIPS if the point is the **real** yield.
3. Stop. Do **not** fill with: HY credit, EM local debt (no pack), leveraged bond ETFs, 52w-low cyclicals, NKE-style turnarounds, autos on China-bear earnings, Indian/US IT-services derates, gold miners, juniors, oil ETFs, fertilizer peak-P/E.

## Look up (URL + date on every rate)

Spending-currency deposit curve (3m, 6m, 12m), CPI, `real_yield = rate − CPI`. Live pack hurdles (12m VND / 10y UST / gilt / Bund / ACGB). FX only if parking off-currency.

## Tenor

Dry powder for a sale-off, not coupon maximization.

- Prefer **3–6m** when the idle book is waiting to buy stocks; review at each maturity.
- **12m** only for money they can lock.
- Do not pick a date the market “must” deflate. Roll until the hold-until rule fires.

## Hold until

An equity name clears QAD **good / very-good** with thesis intact (`core.md` price zones). Not “the index is −20%.” Not “six months passed, buy the dip.” Not FTSE/MSCI upgrade. Trim/break the deposit as needed to fund that buy.

On each maturity / review: re-price the Watch list; NOTIFY on zone entry or thesis break. Rolling deposits without checking zones is a failed routine.

## Output (required)

1. One line: equities empty; ballast (gold/metals/fertilizer/oil/gas) not cheap; waiting-room = **named instrument**.
2. Instrument, **live rate**, tenor, real yield vs CPI, where to buy (e.g. Big-4 online, not counter).
3. Short opportunity-cost table (3–5 rows): this park vs rejected stocks vs gold vs 10y UST / gilt / etc.
4. Hold-until rule + first review = next maturity.
5. Hard avoids.
6. Watch-list hooks: tickers + good/very-good triggers the park will fund (even if BUY=0 this run).

Empty equity + empty ballast **without** this block is a failed run.
