# Overlay: AI / semiconductors

Read **before** scoring valuation when MODE=`AI_OVERLAY`. Can hard-fail a great business. Does not turn a bubble into a bargain.

AI is real. **The price of AI infrastructure is often a cycle.** Do not buy the theme.

## Bucket (pick one)

1. Accelerator / GPU / custom silicon (CUDA lock-in, 4–5 hyperscaler customers)
2. Foundry (process lead, utilization, capex, geopolitics)
3. Wafer equipment / EDA (EUV monopoly vs more cyclical etch/deposition/test)
4. Memory (HBM vs commodity DRAM/NAND — HBM still cycles)
5. Hyperscaler (MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, META, Oracle): AI is capex and depreciation on an ads/cloud/retail **core** — split them
6. Application software “AI feature” — usually not a chip cycle; does AI raise WTP or only opex that copies in 12 months?
7. Power, cooling, networking, OSAT, materials

If the 10-K only uses AI internally, skip this overlay.

## Bubble vs cycle vs franchise

- Franchise: process lead, EUV, CUDA installed base. Can still be too expensive.
- Cycle: capex up 2–3 years then pause. Templates: 1995–2002 fiber, 2017–19 crypto miners, 2020–22 pandemic cloud. “This time is different” is not a valuation method.
- Bubble: price needs years of peak capex, peak margins, and no substitution. A 20% ATH drawdown is usually **down but not cheap**.

Deflation path (must write, or say none exists): **AI use can grow** while this firm’s earnings fall 20–40% via hyperscaler pause, custom ASICs, model efficiency (tokens/watt up, capex/token down), China stack, accelerator competition, or power bottlenecks. That is how the complex deflates — not “AI dies.”

## Checklist (UNKNOWN if missing)

Customer capex 2–3y vs OCF vs D&A (capex >> D&A for years ⇒ supplier earnings above mid-cycle); cancelable RPO/backlog; training vs inference; China / export-control share; top-1 / top-4 concentration; foundry utilization or GPU lead times; GM vs 10y range (haircut records toward 75th percentile, not the last four quarters); capex/sales; reverse DCF vs **through-cycle** growth at local 10y+4–6%. Taiwan/strait; US/NL/JP export rules; power/water/substations. FCF after standstill capex. SBC is real dilution.

## Hard-fail (no Strong buy / Accumulate)

Any one: reverse DCF needs peak hyperscaler capex for 3+ years; top customer ≥ ~25–30% and cutting capex; GM/bookings at a cycle record and multiple still above 10y median; thesis is only “AI TAM”; China share material and unmodeled; foundry/equipment valued on peak utilization/bookings; hyperscaler AI ROIC unproven but fully capitalized.

A fortress foundry or lithography monopoly can still be Watch / Avoid on valuation.

## Soft-fail

Franchise OK, mid-cycle plausible, FCF yield < local 10y+2%; ATH drawdown < 25% with no earnings reset; bull is “multiple re-rating on AI.”

## Pass (rare)

Mid-cycle multiple **and** utilization already mean-reverted, net cash, reverse DCF on mid-cycle wafer starts; or a hyperscaler where you pay for **core** FCF and get AI optionality free (core FCF yield clears the hurdle after maintenance capex). Memory at trough with a fortress balance sheet is usually Cycle-trade, not core accumulation.

## Overlay output (on top of core)

Bucket; cycle position; mid-cycle earnings vs LTM + haircut; reverse DCF implied vs through-cycle growth; 5–8 line deflation path or “no path, moat is X”; customer capex vs D&A; Pass / Soft-fail wait / Hard-fail; if hard-fail, the **one number** that would reverse it.

Do not buy a basket of “AI winners” as diversification — one factor. Cash or a boring non-AI compounder at a real discount beats a great chip franchise at a peak multiple. Hard-fail + user still wants exposure = Cycle-trade only, not long-term accumulation.
