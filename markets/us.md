# MARKET=US

Hurdle: 10-year UST. FCF **after SBC**. Listings: NYSE / Nasdaq common (avoid thin OTC). Filings: 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, DEF 14A, Form 4.

## Dilution and buybacks

RSU + option + convertible = fully diluted. Buyback yield ≈ SBC ⇒ **zero net shrink**. Buybacks above conservative value destroy value. Cheap RSUs + offsetting buybacks are the US twin of VN par-ESOP.

## Banks

CET1, NCO, AOCI holes, HTM vs AFS, deposit beta, CRE. Not “P/E 10.” Through-cycle ROE vs COE.

## Software / platforms

FCF after SBC, net retention, customer concentration. Rule of 40 is marketing. Mentions of Copilot in the 10-K do not make it an AI product.

## Energy / materials

Mid-cycle strip, not LTM. If the “business” is the commodity, prefer `overlays/real-assets.md` unless it is a true first-quartile compounder.

## Index trap

S&P/QQQ/Mag-7 membership is not quality. A 15–20% Mag-7 dip from ATH is usually **down but not cheap**. When the index is concentrated, hunt equal-weight and unloved cash compounders. Mega-caps that already dominate world indices are poor diversifiers for a VND accumulator.

## AI / semis

If the thesis is GPU, foundry, equipment, HBM, or hyperscaler capex: read `overlays/ai-semis.md` before valuation. Do not rotate from fairly priced VN names into NVDA-class names just to “go US.”

## FX

Quote USD, then a rough VND. USD T-bills are a real alternative to stretching US multiples.
