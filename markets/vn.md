# MARKET=VN

Hurdle: 12-month big-4 VND deposit (10y VGB if useful). Listings: HOSE / HNX / UPCOM. Language: Vietnamese filings.

## Filings

BCTC / BCTN, SSC, exchange, AGM resolutions. CafeF / Vietstock / Simplize are **locators**, not the last source. Split-adjust **every** bonus / stock dividend — bonus shares are not value. Par ESOP is real economic dilution (gift ≈ market − par). Pending 10:1 or 15% stock dividends are splits, not crashes.

**Price-zone rebasing (mandatory on GDKHQ / bonus / stock dividend):**
If a Watch/buy hook was set on the **pre-event** quote (e.g. FPT good ≤65k when tape showed ~72k), on ex-date you **must** rebase:
`zone_post = zone_pre / (1 + bonus_ratio)` (10:1 → ÷1.1 → ≤59.1k).
- Ex-rights **reference** ≈ prior_close/(1+ratio) is **not** a sale-off and **not** an automatic zone hit.
- Do **not** NOTIFY or Probe just because the adjusted print sits near the old nominal hook.
- State both: pre-event zone → post-event zone. Compare live price only to the **rebased** zone.
- Same for 52w highs used in “% vs 52w” when the vendor has not adjusted yet — prefer split-adjusted series.

## Banks

Add: credit-growth room, recap via stock dividend vs cash rights, SOE policy lending, LLR club (100%+), group-2, CRE share vs internal caps, related-party (Vin, military, etc.) **quantified in notes or UNKNOWN**. CAR near the floor + a cash raise is a dilution thesis breaker. Do not buy “low P/B” if coverage is leaving the fortress club and CRE is accelerating.

## Tech

FPT/CMG/ELC = IT services / SI / telecom / education. Not an AI-product stock. Japan concentration, FOX deconsolidation (associate profit ≠ CFO), ESOP at par. Compare FCF yield to VND deposits, not to a 2024 30× PE.

## Cyclicals

Steel, fertilizer, chemicals, brokers: mid-cycle, never H1-record NPAT. Property: default avoid unless legal + cash collected + NAV-to-cash is documented. FTSE/MSCI upgrade is not a thesis (T1 is small vs foreign selling).

## Index trap

VNIndex can look cheap while Vin-skewed (high-PE VIC vs cheap banks). Ex-Vin multiples matter. Thin liquidity + 7–9% deposits cap multiples. Selective market ≠ buy the index.

## Sale-off bar

- **Banks only:** Crisis-style **2022 bank** prices (fortress coverage, CRE contained, recap risk known) are the QAD bar for VN banks — not merely “below 52w high.” Do not buy low P/B if coverage is leaving the fortress club and CRE is accelerating.
- **Non-banks (tech, consumer, industrials, etc.):** do **not** require 2022 bank multiples. Use `core.md` price zones + own history + FCF/earnings yield vs **12m VND deposit**, with mid-cycle normalization for cyclicals. A quality VN compounder 20–30% off a bubble multiple is often **down but not cheap**; that sentence is about bubble de-rating, not a license to import the bank crisis bar onto FPT-style names.
- Index trap still applies: VNIndex can look cheap on Vin-skew — ex-Vin and per-name zones matter.
